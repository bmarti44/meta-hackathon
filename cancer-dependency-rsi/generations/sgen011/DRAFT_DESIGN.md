## Design tension to resolve at generation time
The locked Lock-S estimand concerned organoid rank displacement joined to
paired-format lability. The established pool is overwhelmingly adherent cell
lines; only 11 established organoids exist. A fresh evaluation therefore
cannot simply re-run the Lock-S estimand on this pool. Candidate framings
for sol-xhigh to adjudicate (none selected here):
A. Patient-level replication of the lability/displacement DIRECTION on cell
   lines grouped by measured culture environment (growth pattern / media
   formulation strata), with patient as the resampling unit - tests transport
   of the mechanism beyond organoids; estimand differs from Lock-S and must
   be pre-registered as new, not a re-test of C.
B. Organoid-restricted design conditional on resolving the 97 unresolved
   organoids' identities (requires an identity resource beyond the two
   supplied files - e.g. Passports API receipts like pgen003, or user-supplied
   crosswalk); power at ~100 organoid patients needs assessment.
C. Two-stage: (i) metadata-only power/identifiability generation comparing A
   and B on measurable strata; (ii) locked outcome generation only for the
   surviving framing.
