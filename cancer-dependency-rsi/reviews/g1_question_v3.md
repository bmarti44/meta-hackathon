## The single question
**Does measured format-bundle lability of a gene's dependency (Broad controlled panel) predict how badly that gene's dependency ranking transfers to independent Sanger organoids — or can any such association be bounded below decision relevance?**

## Decision rules (disjoint; two-sided bound)
- `SIGNAL`: confirmatory 95% CI for partial ρ entirely **above +δ**.
- `BOUND` (informative refutation): CI entirely **within (−δ, +δ)** — excludes decision-relevant association in *both* directions.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: otherwise. Reported as such; no reinterpretation.
**δ candidate = 0.20, justification (decision-linked, Lock 1 ratifies or replaces with stated reasoning):** the endpoint exists to decide whether lability should *triage target trust* (deprioritize high-lability targets from 2D evidence). A rank association of 0.20 corresponds to lability ordering agreeing with displacement ordering only marginally better than chance for pairwise target comparisons (Kendall-equivalent ≈ 0.13, ~57% pairwise concordance) — below any threshold at which a triage rule would change shortlist composition in practice. δ is *not* derived from our exploratory point estimate (0.055) or from S3's different correlation type; Lock 1 must re-justify or amend it with the same decision argument before outcomes are touched.
